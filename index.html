❓ How to use FreezeFix

🧊 Unfreeze this
Highlight text on any page, right-click → Unfreeze this. Or paste it in the box, or press Scan this page. You get a one-line gist and one step at a time: Done ✓, Too big, Skip. Shortcuts on a step: D, B, S.
⚡ 1. Brain Dump
Type any stray thought and press Enter. Gets it out of your head without breaking flow.
🧩 2. Break Down Tasks
Stuck on a big task? Click Break Down to instantly split it into 2-minute starter steps.
➕ 3. More
Streaks, Day Flow timeline & auto-schedule, calendar import, history and "What next?" are tucked under More at the bottom of the Focus view.
🆘 4. "Stuck?" Rescue
Paralyzed on a task? Tap 🆘 Stuck? on your focus card for an instant 30-second starter move.
⌨️ Toggle Side Panel: Alt + Shift + F
